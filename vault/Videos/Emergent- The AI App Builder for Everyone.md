Emergent is building the next generation of app development, where anyone can create software with natural language. The platform lets users describe what they want, and Emergent’s AI builds it — from web apps to automations to tools used by millions.

In this interview with YC Partner Nicolas Dessaigne, co-founders Mukhund and Madhav Jha share how they scaled to $15M ARR in just three months, what inspired them to make app creation accessible to everyone, and how they see a future where building software is as easy as having an idea.
