## YouTube Description

Weave (YC W25) helps engineering teams understand whether their AI coding tools are actually making them more productive. Its models measure the work humans and agents produce and compare that output with AI spending. The company recently raised a $13.5M Series A led by Standard Capital and launched Weave Router, which selects models for different coding tasks.

In this Fireside, co-founders Adam Cohen and Andrew Churchill discuss how they went from measuring human productivity to optimizing coding agents, why cost per output matters more than raw token spend, and how their AI coach helps engineers work more effectively with agents. They also share their vision for autonomous software factories and explain why combining different models’ strengths could outperform any single model.
