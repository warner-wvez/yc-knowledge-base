## YouTube Description

Aleph is building the AI platform for modern finance — one that meets teams where they already work: in spreadsheets. By combining the flexibility of a spreadsheet with the intelligence of AI, Aleph helps companies automate forecasting, reporting, and planning in real time.

In this interview with YC Partner Aaron Epstein, co-founders Albert Gozzi and Santiago Perez De Rosso share how they’re rethinking FP&A from first principles, what it’s like building software for some of the world’s fastest-growing companies, and how Aleph scaled to a $29M Series B led by Khosla Ventures, with customers like Zapier, Turo, and Harvey.
