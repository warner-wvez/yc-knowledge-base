## YouTube Description

Greptile is building AI that understands entire codebases. Each week, Greptile reviews 5–8 million lines of code for customers and surfaces tens of thousands of bugs that would otherwise slip through human review.

Fresh off a $25M Series A round, they're on a mission to kill the bug.

In an interview with YC Partner Brad Flora, co-founder and CEO Daksh Gupta shares how he and his team discovered that large language models are often better at reading code than writing it, why code review is the most powerful use case for that ability, and how Greptile is helping developers spend less time catching bugs and more time creating software.

Learn more about Greptile: https://www.greptile.com
