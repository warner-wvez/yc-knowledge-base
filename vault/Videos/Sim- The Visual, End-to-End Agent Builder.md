## YouTube Description

Sim is building the visual, end-to-end agent builder — a platform where developers can design, test, and deploy real AI agents that work in production.

Founded by Emir Karabeg and Waleed Latif, Sim grew from a small San Francisco apartment to a community of 60,000 developers and 18,000 GitHub stars, recently raising a $7M Series A.

In this interview with YC’s Aaron Epstein, Emir shares how they’re building the infrastructure for the agent era, and what it takes to create AI systems that reason, retrieve, and act safely at scale.

Learn more about Sim at https://www.sim.ai.
