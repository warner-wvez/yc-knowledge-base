## YouTube Description

Confido (YC S21) is building the AI workforce for consumer brands, automating everything from accounting to sales and demand planning. They work with brands like Olipop and Dude Wipes, as well as divisions of Mars and Unilever, and recently raised a $55 million Series B led by Insight.

In this episode of Founder Firesides, Confido co-founders Justin Hunter and Kara Holinski join YC’s Diana Hu to discuss escaping pivot hell, landing their first customer with a Figma prototype, and winning seven-figure deals for AI that does the work. They also explain how they build without product managers.
