## YouTube Description

Stepful (YC S21) trains people for healthcare jobs, combining AI-supported learning with local hands-on training. The company trained 30,000 people last year and now works with 39 health systems, including seven of the 10 largest in the U.S. It recently raised a $55 million Series C led by Oak HC/FT.

In this episode of Founder Firesides, YC Managing Partner Diana Hu hosts co-founders Carl Madi and Tressia Hobeika where they share how cleaners losing their jobs during the pandemic sparked the idea for a faster, more affordable path into healthcare. They discuss preparing students for medical assistant jobs in five months instead of two years, using AI tutors to personalize learning, and helping hospitals train workers without asking them to leave their communities.
