## YouTube Description

Candid Health is rebuilding the medical billing stack for healthcare providers, automating the work required to get claims submitted correctly and providers paid. 

Medical billing is a $200–300 billion market, and much of it still runs on decades-old software and manual processes. Today, Candid serves more than 200 healthcare organizations, processes about $7 billion in claims each year, and recently raised a $120 million Series D to keep expanding into the broader healthcare market.

In this Founder Fireside, co-founder and CEO Nick Perry joins YC's Gustaf Alströmer to share how Candid went from manually reviewing every claim to building the automation layer behind the business. He also explains where AI and agents fit into medical billing, and why the goal is to prevent denials before they happen.
