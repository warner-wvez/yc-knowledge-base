## YouTube Description

HappyRobot is building the digital workforce for the real world — AI agents that power the operations behind global companies like DHL, Uber Freight, and Flexport. Less than a year after their Series A, they’ve grown revenue 10x and just raised a $44M Series B.

In this interview with YC General Partner Diana Hu, the founders share their journey from an early YC pivot to reinventing logistics, why voice was the hardest problem worth solving, and how they’re automating the invisible work that keeps goods, people, and services moving around the world.
