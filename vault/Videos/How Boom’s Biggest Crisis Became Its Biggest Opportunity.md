## Summary

The Social Radars Podcast: Boom founder and CEO Blake Scholl returns to The Social Radars to tell the story of how that crisis became one of the company’s biggest opportunities: gas turbines.

## YouTube Description

In this Social Radars episode we talk with Blake Scholl about one of the cleverest business model transformations in the history of startups: Boom's move into building gas turbines. When Rolls Royce pulled out as Boom's engine supplier, it seemed to many like fatally bad news. But instead it not only helped Boom solve boomless supersonic flight, but also enabled them to tap a huge new revenue stream to fund development of the airliner.
