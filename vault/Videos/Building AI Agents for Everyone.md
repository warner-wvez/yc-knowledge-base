## YouTube Description

Gumloop (YC W24) is an AI agent builder used by companies like Shopify, Gusto, and Instacart. It lets employees build and share agents that automate work across their existing tools, while IT controls security, data access, and costs.

In this Fireside, co-founders Max Brodeur-Urbas and Rahul Behal talk to Y Combinator General Partner Gustaf Alströmer share how better models transformed their visual workflow builder into an agent platform, what they learned from 1,100 customer calls, and how one power user helped them land Instacart. They also explain why bringing agents into tools like Slack drives adoption and why the people who understand a task should be the ones automating it—to help teams do more, not simply replace them.
